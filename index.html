    About UPInspect

    Adding Control to QR-Based Payments

    UPInspect is a privacy-first utility that decodes UPI QR codes before initiating a transaction. Instead of blindly redirecting you to a payment app, UPInspect adds a transparent verification step so you can review the UPI ID, merchant name and requested amount, putting you completely in control of your digital payments.

    Features

    • Decode & InspectExtract and view hidden details - UPI ID, merchant name, and requested amount from any QR code before paying.
    • Scan or UploadUse your device's camera for live scanning, or upload a saved QR screenshot directly from your gallery.
    • Create & ShareGenerate custom UPI payment links and professional QR standee cards you can share anywhere.
    • 100% Private & LocalYour payment data never leaves your device. Everything runs securely within your browser, no servers, no tracking.

    Usages

    • Fraud PreventionVerify the actual payee identity before confirming a transfer, never pay blind again.
    • Manual Banking TransfersCopy the extracted UPI ID to handle higher-value payments through your bank's official app.
    • Remote PaymentsSafely inspect and pay for shared QR screenshots received via WhatsApp or other chat apps.
    • For Freelancers & MerchantsCreate clean, shareable payment links without exposing your personal phone number.

    How to pay with Copy UPI ID

    Use this when Pay Now is declined, or when you want to pay more than a link allows. First tap Copy UPI ID on the result card, then follow the steps for your app.

    Google Pay
    1. Open Google Pay and tap the search bar at the top of the home screen.
    2. Paste the copied UPI ID and select the result that appears.
    3. Check the name shown, then tap Pay.
    4. Enter the amount and tap Proceed.
    5. Choose your bank account and enter your UPI PIN.
    PhonePe
    1. Open PhonePe and tap To Bank/UPI ID under Transfer Money.
    2. Paste the copied UPI ID and tap Verify.
    3. Check the name shown for the ID.
    4. Enter the amount and tap Send or Pay.
    5. Enter your UPI PIN.
    Paytm
    1. Open Paytm and tap To UPI ID or Bank A/c on the home screen. It may read To Bank or UPI ID.
    2. Choose the UPI ID option and paste the copied ID.
    3. Check the name shown for the ID.
    4. Enter the amount and tap Proceed.
    5. Enter your UPI PIN.
    BHIM
    1. Open BHIM and tap Send on the home screen.
    2. Paste the copied UPI ID and tap Verify.
    3. Check the name shown for the ID.
    4. Enter the amount and tap Pay.
    5. Enter your UPI PIN.
    Other UPI and bank apps
    1. Open your app and look for Pay, Send Money or To UPI ID.
    2. Paste the copied UPI ID and check the name shown.
    3. Enter the amount.
    4. Confirm with your UPI PIN.

    Before you enter your UPI PIN, check that the name your app shows for the UPI ID matches who you mean to pay. Unlike the name in a QR code, that name comes from the payee's bank.

    Menu names change between app versions. If you cannot find an option, look for Pay, Send or To UPI ID.

    FAQs

    What does UPInspect actually do?

    UPInspect decodes UPI QR codes and displays the embedded payment details before you initiate the transaction. You can then pay via your preferred UPI app, copy the UPI ID to send manually, or generate a clean payment link.

    Does UPInspect process payments?

    No. All payments are completed within your selected UPI-enabled banking or payment app. UPInspect does not handle or store funds.

    Why do some QR payments show a ₹2,000 limit?

    In certain cases, banks or UPI apps may apply transaction caps or additional checks when payments are initiated directly through QR scan flows. These limits are determined by your bank or UPI provider, not by UPInspect.

    Does UPInspect bypass the ₹2,000 QR limit?

    No. UPInspect does not bypass, override, or interfere with any bank-imposed limits. All transactions remain fully subject to your bank's rules, authentication requirements, and regulatory guidelines.

    Then how does UPInspect help with higher-value payments?

    UPInspect allows you to extract the UPI ID from a QR code and initiate payment manually through your bank's standard transfer flow. Any limits, approvals, or security checks are still enforced entirely by your bank. UPInspect only gives you flexibility in how you start the payment.

    Is UPInspect compliant with UPI rules?

    Yes. UPInspect does not alter transaction data or modify payment limits. It only decodes publicly embedded UPI QR information and allows users to proceed through official UPI apps. All transactions follow NPCI guidelines and your bank's policies.

    Pay Now was declined by my UPI app. What should I do?

    Some UPI apps stop payments that start from links they do not recognise, to protect you from fraud. That is a safety check in your app, not a fault in UPInspect. Tap Copy UPI ID, open your payment app, paste the ID into its pay-to-UPI-ID screen and enter the amount yourself. Steps for popular apps are in the guide above.

    Does Copy UPI ID also have the ₹2,000 limit?

    Caps such as ₹2,000 are usually applied to link and QR payment flows. When you paste a UPI ID and type the amount inside your payment app, it is a normal payment, so you can pay any amount up to the limits of your own bank account and UPI app.

    Is Copy UPI ID more reliable than Pay Now?

    Yes. Pay Now passes the details to your UPI app as a link, and some apps refuse to act on links. Copy UPI ID works like typing the ID yourself, so your app treats it as a normal payment. Before you enter your UPI PIN, check that the name your app shows for the ID matches who you mean to pay.
